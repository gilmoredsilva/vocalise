Vocalise — SVM Experiment 8
============================

Experiment:
Added MFCC delta and delta-delta temporal features to the 38-feature baseline.

Dataset:
CREMA-D
Total recordings: 7,442
Actors: 91
Classes: 6

Evaluation:
Speaker-independent train/test split
Test samples: 1,552
No actor overlap between train and test
Standardization: Training data only

Features:
38-feature baseline
+ 13 MFCC delta means
+ 13 MFCC delta standard deviations
+ 13 MFCC delta-delta means
+ 13 MFCC delta-delta standard deviations

Total ML features: 64

Model:
SVM with RBF kernel

Results:
Accuracy: 50.90%
Macro F1: 0.50

Class F1:
Angry: 0.73
Disgust: 0.39
Fear: 0.41
Happy: 0.47
Neutral: 0.48
Sad: 0.53

Comparison:
Baseline accuracy: 50.19%
MFCC delta/delta-delta accuracy: 50.90%

Accuracy improvement: +0.71 percentage points

Observation:
MFCC temporal derivatives produced a modest improvement in accuracy,
with the largest improvement observed for the angry class. However,
overall Macro F1 remained approximately unchanged, and performance
remained below the spectral contrast experiment.
