Vocalise — SVM Experiment 7
============================

Experiment:
Added spectral flatness mean and standard deviation to the 38-feature baseline.

Dataset:
CREMA-D
Total recordings: 7,442
Actors: 91
Classes: 6

Evaluation:
Speaker-independent train/test split
Test samples: 1,552
No actor overlap between train and test
Standardization: Training data only

Features:
38-feature baseline + 2 spectral flatness features

Total ML features: 40

Model:
SVM with RBF kernel

Results:
Accuracy: 50.39%
Macro F1: 0.50

Class F1:
Angry: 0.70
Disgust: 0.38
Fear: 0.46
Happy: 0.44
Neutral: 0.46
Sad: 0.56

Comparison:
Baseline accuracy: 50.19%
Spectral flatness accuracy: 50.39%

Accuracy change: +0.20 percentage points

Observation:
Spectral flatness produced only a negligible improvement and did not
meaningfully improve Macro F1. It therefore does not appear to provide
substantial additional discriminative information for this configuration.
