Vocalise — SVM Experiment 6
============================

Experiment:
Added spectral flux mean and standard deviation to the 38-feature baseline.

Dataset:
CREMA-D
Total recordings: 7,442
Actors: 91
Classes: 6

Evaluation:
Speaker-independent train/test split
Test samples: 1,552
No actor overlap between train and test
Standardization: Training data only

Features:
38-feature baseline + 2 spectral flux features
- Spectral flux mean
- Spectral flux standard deviation

Total ML features: 40

Model:
SVM with RBF kernel

Results:
Accuracy: 51.68%
Macro F1: 0.51

Class F1:
Angry: 0.70
Disgust: 0.39
Fear: 0.47
Happy: 0.45
Neutral: 0.50
Sad: 0.58

Confusion Matrix:
[[190, 26,  9, 32,  6,  2],
 [ 29,100, 22, 45, 25, 44],
 [ 22, 16,113, 38, 20, 56],
 [ 35, 32, 39,125, 24, 10],
 [  1, 40, 18, 31,111, 26],
 [  2, 31, 20, 17, 32,163]]

Comparison:
Baseline accuracy: 50.19%
Spectral flux accuracy: 51.68%

Accuracy improvement: +1.49 percentage points

Observation:
Adding spectral flux produced a modest improvement over the 38-feature
baseline. This suggests that temporal spectral variation contains useful
information for emotion classification, although its contribution is
smaller than that of spectral contrast.
