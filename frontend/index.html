06 / Research context

Why compare them?

Vocalise places two philosophies of speech emotion recognition beside each other: an inspectable signal-processing pipeline and a representation-learning approach based on DistilHuBERT.

Traditional DSP

Easy to inspect and connect to signal-processing concepts, but limited by the selected handcrafted representation and classical model.

DistilHuBERT

Can learn richer patterns from speech, with more computational cost and less direct interpretability.

Important distinction

Prediction confidence describes this recording. Test accuracy describes evaluation across a dataset; they are not the same measurement.
